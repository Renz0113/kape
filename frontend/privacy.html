Privacy Policy

By creating or accessing your Kapeling Ka account, you acknowledge that the information you provide may be collected and processed to manage your account, process orders, provide customer support, improve our services, and maintain the security of the platform.

If you choose to save a delivery map pin, its latitude and longitude are stored with your account and copied to delivery orders so the team can locate the drop-off point. Device location is requested only after you select “Use my location” and grant browser permission. You can also place a pin manually or continue with your written address.

The map uses OpenStreetMap tiles. When the map loads, your browser connects to OpenStreetMap's tile servers to retrieve map imagery. This notice describes the location features currently provided by this site.

Your information will be handled in accordance with this notice and the purposes described above.
